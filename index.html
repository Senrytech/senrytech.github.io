Laser Equipment Purchasing Pitfall Guide | Make every penny of your investment worthwhile

Accurate avoidance selection、operate、Core issues in production，Reduce trial and error costs

01

low price gimmick，Core component laser power virtual standard+Substituting one thing for another

Low prices attract purchases，Replace the original laser with an off-brand imitation or high-imitation model，Even confusing virtual power by model number；There are even unscrupulous merchants who replace original lasers with second-hand refurbished parts to make profits.！

02

Pay a high price but get a low-end laser head，How to choose the cutting head that suits you？

One is to use second-hand refurbished cutting heads to pass them off as original brand new accessories.；The second is to use high-priced but low-end cutting heads as shoddy ones.。

03

Whole machine warranty vs Core component warranty？

Many manufacturers only guarantee the complete machine，No guarantee of core fragile parts，It must be written into the contract。

04

Simple packaging ≠ save money

How many tons does the machine tool have?～More than ten tons，The ground is soft、The floor slab will collapse due to insufficient load-bearing capacity.。
